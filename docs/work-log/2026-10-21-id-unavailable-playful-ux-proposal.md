## Proposal, not an approved design decision
Concept: FoxMind as a learning workshop, cheerful but not childish. Preserve orange identity, navy text, existing top navigation and classroom-scoped roles. Extend earlier lavender/glass direction with warm ivory background, limited mint and yellow illustration accents; keep reading/forms opaque. Use playful illustrated covers from the predefined catalog, a small family of fox poses and contextual empty states. Avoid toy-like styling on every control.

Candidate palette: background #FFF9F3, brand orange #FF8A3D, action orange #C2410C with white text, lavender #EEE7FF, mint #DDF4E8, navy #20243A. These are candidates; real composited contrast and interaction states are not validated.

Home: compact greeting and fox illustration, prominent truthful next action, Enseño and Estudio blocks, real classroom updates. Keep no-data states; do not invent tasks, progress or achievements. Classroom cards have coherent illustrated identities and existing metadata. Plan uses unit/topic milestones with text states, not a new gamification system. Assessments reduce decoration. Forms retain existing steps and saved-state truth. Progress distinguishes submissions, published grades and taught topics.

No rankings, streaks, points, AI assistant or global teacher/student switch. Motion is brief, meaningful and reduced-motion compatible; no continuous decorative animation. Proposed first visual validation: Inicio, one classroom view and one account screen, desktop/mobile, before authorizing implementation. Broader classroom styling is conceptual only and does not authorize integration.
